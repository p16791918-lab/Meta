**12. Review question**
In the general female population, how does the incidence of invasive breast
cancer (overall and by molecular subtype) differ between racial/ethnic minority
groups (Black/African-American, Hispanic/Latina, Asian/Pacific Islander,
American Indian/Alaska Native) and non-Hispanic White women, expressed as
age-adjusted/age-standardized incidence rates and incidence rate ratios (IRR)?

**13. Searches**
PubMed (MEDLINE), Embase, Scopus, and Web of Science Core Collection will be
searched from 2000-01-01 to 2025-12-31, limited to English-language,
human studies. The strategy combines three concept blocks — (1) breast cancer,
(2) race/ethnicity/disparity required in the title, (3) incidence /
age-adjusted or age-standardized rate — with database-specific syntax, and
excludes reviews, case reports, editorials, letters and notes. Full search
strings for all four databases are in the study record (see
"URL to search strategy"). Reference lists of included studies and relevant
reviews will be hand-searched. No date/geography restriction beyond the above.

**15. Condition or domain being studied**
Invasive breast cancer (female); descriptive cancer epidemiology; racial and
ethnic health disparities in cancer incidence.

**16. Participants / population**
Women in the general population, grouped by race/ethnicity (non-Hispanic White
[reference], Black/African-American, Hispanic/Latina, Asian/Pacific Islander,
American Indian/Alaska Native, and disaggregated subgroups where reported).
Male breast cancer is excluded.

**17. Intervention(s) / exposure(s)**
Racial/ethnic minority group membership (the "exposure" in this
disparities/observational framing).

**18. Comparator(s) / control**
Non-Hispanic White women.

**19. Types of study to be included**
Observational, population-based studies: cohort, cross-sectional, and cancer
registry / population-based studies reporting age-adjusted or age-standardized
invasive breast-cancer incidence rates by race/ethnicity. Excluded: reviews,
case reports/series (n<10), studies reporting only mortality, survival, stage
or treatment (no incidence), prevalence-only studies, DCIS-only studies,
studies of male breast cancer, animal/in-vitro studies, and studies reporting
only crude (non-age-adjusted) rates (crude-only handled in sensitivity analysis).

**20. Context**
General-population incidence, any country/registry; primary analyses will
consider setting (US SEER/USCS vs other) given screening-access differences.

**21. Main outcome(s)**
Age-adjusted / age-standardized incidence rate of **invasive breast cancer**
(per 100,000) and the incidence rate ratio (IRR) of each minority group vs
non-Hispanic White women.
*Measures of effect:* incidence rate ratio (log IRR with standard error), pooled
by random-effects meta-analysis.

**22. Additional outcome(s)**
Subtype-specific incidence and IRR — triple-negative (TNBC), ER/PR/HER2-defined
subtypes; age-specific incidence and the black–white age crossover; temporal
trends (annual percentage change) where reported.

**23. Data extraction (selection and coding)**
Two reviewers will independently screen titles/abstracts then full texts against
the criteria; disagreements resolved by discussion/third reviewer. For each
included study, two reviewers will extract: citation, country/registry, period,
age range, race/ethnicity groups, case counts, person-years or population
denominators, age-adjusted incidence rates with 95% CI, standard population used,
subtype definitions, and IRRs where reported. Data recorded in a piloted
extraction form.

**24. Risk of bias (quality) assessment**
Study quality/risk of bias assessed independently by two reviewers using the
Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) critical-appraisal checklist for studies reporting
prevalence/incidence data, supplemented by the Newcastle–Ottawa Scale for
cohort components. Domains: representativeness of the population, case
ascertainment/registry completeness, appropriateness of the standard population
for age adjustment, and completeness of race/ethnicity classification.

**25. Strategy for data synthesis**
Random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird) meta-analysis of log IRRs (minority vs
non-Hispanic White), pooled separately by minority group and by outcome
(overall invasive vs TNBC/subtype). Heterogeneity by I², τ² and Cochran's Q,
with 95% prediction intervals. Where studies report rates for only one group,
they are summarized narratively (not pooled). Because several US studies draw on
overlapping registries (SEER/NPCR), a key sensitivity analysis will restrict to
non-overlapping data sources / one estimate per registry-period to avoid
double-counting; independence limitations will be reported. Publication/small-
study bias examined with funnel plots and Egger's test where ≥10 studies.
If pooling is inappropriate, findings synthesized narratively (SWiM).

**26. Analysis of subgroups or subsets**
By racial/ethnic group; by molecular subtype (TNBC, HR+/HER2−, etc.); by age
(<50 vs ≥50; young-onset crossover); by setting/country (US SEER/USCS vs other);
by calendar period; and by disaggregated Asian/Hispanic subgroups where data
allow.

**27. Type and method of review**
Systematic review with meta-analysis (quantitative synthesis) of observational
incidence data. Epidemiologic / prevalence-incidence review.
