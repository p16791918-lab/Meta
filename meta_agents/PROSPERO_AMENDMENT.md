## 갱신 필드 (아래 영어 문안을 각 칸에 붙여넣기)

### Review title
Racial and Ethnic Differences in Breast Cancer Incidence in the United States: A
Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

### Review objectives
To systematically review and meta-analyze US population-based studies reporting
invasive female breast cancer incidence by race and ethnicity relative to
non-Hispanic White women, and to compare aggregate race/ethnicity estimates with
estimates disaggregated by detailed ethnicity (e.g., Asian American and Pacific
Islander subgroups), molecular subtype, age, and nativity.

### Searches
PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, and Web of Science Core Collection will be searched
from 1 January 2000 to the search date in 2026, limited to English-language human studies.
Three concept blocks (breast cancer; race/ethnicity/disparity; incidence/age-
adjusted rate) will be combined with a US-context block, using title/abstract/
keyword fields and controlled vocabulary. Reviews, letters, editorials, notes, and
conference abstracts will be excluded. Reference lists of included studies will be
screened. Full database-specific strings are provided in a supplementary appendix.

### Study design
Observational population-based/registry studies (cross-sectional incidence and
registry trend analyses) reporting age-adjusted incidence rates or rate ratios.

### Condition or domain being studied
Invasive female breast cancer incidence (age-adjusted) in the United States, and
its variation by race and ethnicity.

### Population
Population-based (cancer-registry) female populations in the United States.
Inclusion: studies reporting age-adjusted invasive breast cancer incidence for at
least one US racial/ethnic minority group relative to non-Hispanic White women.
Exclusion: mortality, survival, prevalence, in-situ-only, stage-specific-only,
male breast cancer, mortality-to-incidence ratios, and non-US studies.

### Intervention(s) or exposure(s)
Race and ethnicity (self-identified or registry-classified), including
disaggregated ethnic subgroups, molecular subtype, nativity, and age band.

### Comparator(s) or control(s)
Non-Hispanic White women (reference group).

### Main outcomes
Within-study incidence rate ratio (IRR) = minority age-adjusted incidence ÷
non-Hispanic White age-adjusted incidence (age-adjusted rate per 100,000; IRR with
95% CI).

### Measures of effect
Incidence rate ratio (IRR); reported rate ratio.

### Additional outcomes
Subtype-specific IRRs (HR+/HER2−, HR+/HER2+, HER2-enriched, triple-negative,
ER-status); age-band-specific rate ratios; nativity-stratified IRRs; disaggregated
Asian/Pacific Islander ethnic-subgroup IRRs.

### Data extraction (selection and coding)
*(DECISION 1 — choose one)*
**(b) single reviewer + AI:** One reviewer will screen titles/abstracts then full
texts and extract data, with AI-assisted support for retrieval, de-duplication, and
first-pass classification; all screening decisions and every extracted value will
be verified by the reviewer against the source article. Items: age-adjusted rates,
IRR/RR, 95% CI or case counts/person-years, registry, region, calendar period,
race/ethnicity categories, subtype, age band.
**(a) two reviewers:** Two reviewers will independently screen and extract data,
resolving disagreements by consensus; each value verified against the source
article. (Use this only if a second reviewer will actually do it.)

### Risk of bias (quality) assessment
Risk of bias of included observational studies will be assessed with the
Newcastle–Ottawa Scale. *(변경 없음 — 2023 기록 그대로 유지)*

### Strategy for data synthesis
Random-effects meta-analysis of within-study log-IRRs (restricted maximum
likelihood; Hartung–Knapp adjustment in sensitivity analysis), with I², τ², and
95% prediction intervals. Because US cancer registries overlap (SEER ⊂ NAACCR ⊂
USCS; county ⊂ state ⊂ SEER), one estimate per registry family will be used in the
primary analysis, with all-studies and overlap-excluded analyses as sensitivity
checks. Publication/small-study bias assessed qualitatively (funnel/Egger only if
≥10 studies). Certainty of evidence via GRADE.

### Analysis of subgroups or subsets
By detailed ethnicity (Asian/Pacific Islander subgroups), molecular subtype, age
band, and nativity. *(2023의 "Not applicable"에서 변경)*
