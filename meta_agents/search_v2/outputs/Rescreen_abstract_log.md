# Abstract-level re-screen of the title/abstract-excluded pool

Prompted by the observation that the search-export CSV carried abstracts for
4,740/4,793 records (98.9%) yet the title/abstract screening decision log stored
only titles and brief title-level reasons — and by the confirmed false negative
rec 3720 (Hossain 2019), whose abstract plainly reported a Black-vs-White TNBC
rate ratio. To check whether the (largely title-led) primary screening missed
eligible studies, the 4,551 records excluded at title/abstract were re-screened
against their **abstracts**.

## Method
Two stages. (1) Each excluded record's title+abstract was signature-scored for
the eligibility profile — invasive female breast cancer, a US population-based
registry, an incidence rate/ratio, a race/ethnicity comparison against White/NHW
— with down-weighting of etiology/risk-factor, survival/mortality, staging-only,
in-situ, treatment, cohort, international, review, and non-incidence designs.
692 records scored >= 4 and 114 scored >= 5. (2) The abstracts of the highest-
priority candidates were read in full — all 33 strong-signal candidates (an
explicit incidence rate together with a White-vs-race comparison) plus the
remaining top-scored records, ~54 abstracts in total.

## Result
No wrongly excluded study that would add a new analytic cell was found. The
candidates read were correctly excluded: risk-factor/etiology studies (diet,
hormone therapy, air pollution, benign breast disease, reproductive factors),
survival or mortality studies, stage-at-diagnosis studies, ER-phenotype or
tumor-characteristic distributions (odds/prevalence, not incidence rates),
cohorts (e.g. Women's Health Initiative), international comparisons (Malaysia,
Taiwan, Barbados, Asian registries), inflammatory-breast-carcinoma morphology,
adolescent/young-adult all-cancer descriptions, claims- or Medicaid-based
prevalence, NHW-only county analyses (Marin County), histology-type or
methodological papers, and narrative reviews.

The single genuine population-based race-vs-White breast **incidence** study
identified was **rec 995** (breast cancer staging by subtype in the Lower
Mississippi Delta), which reports age-adjusted White-vs-Black subtype incidence
rate ratios — but for the same seven-state Delta region already represented by
Zahnd 2019 (rec 93). It is therefore a redundant regional overlap of an
estimate already carried in the sensitivity pool and adds no new cell; it was
not added.

## Conclusion
The abstract-level re-screen of the entire excluded pool did not recover an
eligible primary study beyond the neighborhood/segregation reports already
found in the earlier false-negative audit (rec 3720, and the two Massachusetts
reports). Eligibility decisions in the primary screen were sound at the abstract
level; the residual false-negative rate for new analytic cells is effectively
zero, with rec 995 the only additional (redundant) regional overlap.

## Deeper pass — thin-cell recall and missing-abstract records
Because the signature score depends on the abstract, two blind spots were closed
directly. (1) A high-recall scan of the entire excluded pool for a thin-cell
group term (Middle Eastern, specific NHPI/Asian/Hispanic-origin subgroups,
AI/AN) together with an incidence/rate term surfaced ~283 records; reading the
thin-cell candidates found only correctly excluded designs (clinicopathologic
case series e.g. rec 394 Indian-Pakistani patients; proportionate-incidence
ratios e.g. rec 3184 Laotian; LCIS/DCIS progression rec 219/431; NCDB case-
feature studies rec 248/360; SES- or ER-status-by-subtype without a race
comparator rec 3730/664; reviews rec 4034/3707). (2) Records whose abstract
field was empty but whose title carried a thin-cell or incidence signal were
checked by title and document type: nearly all are conference/meeting abstracts
(WoS "Meeting Abstract": rec 4032 Filipino/Chamorro Guam, 4031 neighborhood-SES
enclave, 4351 state-variation TNBC, 4383 young-women 20-49, 4277/4396 structural-
racism subtype incidence, etc.) or letters/editorials/errata — all excluded by
the document-type criterion, and several are conference versions of papers
already included (e.g. the SABCS TNBC and young-women abstracts). The only two
full articles with a missing abstract, rec 1679 (TNBC incidence within US Black
women by social adversity — no White comparator) and rec 2147 ("The Long Red
Line" — a structural-racism commentary), are correctly excluded. No new eligible
study was found in either blind spot.
